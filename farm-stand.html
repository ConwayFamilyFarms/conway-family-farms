Visit the Farm Stand

South of Molalla, Oregon. Come buy something fresh and say hello to the flock.

Finding us

HoursOpen 24 hours a day, every day — roadside and self-serve
WhereSouth of Molalla, Oregon [street address & directions to be added]
What’s hereFarm eggs, raw honey, and whatever the garden is producing this week
PaymentCash and card welcome

The stand sits at the roadside and never closes — swing by any time and help yourself. We stock it as the harvest comes in, so what’s on the table is the freshest thing on the farm right now.

Egg customers: the stand is the pickup point for dozens ordered ahead through the contact page.

What to expect

Seasonal, honestly

The stand table changes with the garden. When something’s gone, it’s gone until the next planting — we don’t truck in produce to fill gaps.

Eggs & honey, most weeks

The staples travel with us: dozens from the pastured flock and jars of raw honey from our own hives.

Ask us anything

How the animals are raised, when hog shares open, what’s planting next — the stand is the easiest place to talk farm. For hog shares specifically, start on the shares page.

Pasture-raised eggs, pork, and honey from a family farm on 13 acres south of Molalla, Oregon. Regenerative and no-till since 2023.
