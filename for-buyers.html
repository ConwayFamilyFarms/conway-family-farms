What we raise, and how

Pasture-based, regenerative pork from a 13-acre family farm south of Molalla. Our herd is moving toward Duroc-based genetics specifically for a consistent animal and a consistent product your customers can count on, batch after batch.

CertificationProp 12 certified — every animal movement recorded (we run our own record-keeping software, CFFOS)
PracticesPasture-raised, rotationally managed; regenerative, no-till farm since 2023
Contract workUSDA/NRCS projects on pasture management & rotation, high tunnels, and hedgerows
ProcessingOur own animals are processed through Mt. Angel Meat (Eric), Mt. Angel, Oregon
