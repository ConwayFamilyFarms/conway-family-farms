Everything here is raised or made on this farm. We sell pork the whole-animal way — by the share, not by the cut — so our hogs go from pasture to your freezer without ever sitting in ours.

Whole & Half Hog Shares — Our Main Offer

Reserve a whole or half hog, raised on pasture here and processed at Mt. Angel Meat. Priced per pound on hanging weight; your deposit is credited to your final invoice, and you choose your own cuts.

Deposit: $250 for a whole hog · $150 for a half — holds your share.
